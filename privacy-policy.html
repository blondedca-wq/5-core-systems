Privacy Policy

SecondRing (AI Trade Business OS) - Last updated: August 17, 2026

SecondRing provides communication and operations software for trade businesses. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights. It applies to our websites, client dashboards, and messaging integrations (SMS, phone, email, Instagram, Facebook).

What we collect

Contact details (name, phone number, email), message and call content sent to or from a business that uses SecondRing, job and appointment details a customer provides, and technical logs needed to operate the service. When a business connects its Instagram account, we receive that account's basic profile information and direct messages sent to it.

How we use it

Solely to operate the service for the connected business: identifying inquiries, replying to messages, booking appointments, producing quotes and records, and escalating conversations to a human. Message text may be processed by AI language-model services to classify intent and draft replies. We do not sell personal information, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train AI models.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a Canadian-hosted Postgres database, encrypted at rest and in transit, with per-business access isolation. Access is limited to the business you contacted and to SecondRing personnel who maintain the service. Nightly backups are retained on a rolling basis.

Sharing

Data is shared only with the service providers required to run the platform (hosting, telephony, AI processing) under their own confidentiality obligations, and with the business you communicated with. Never across businesses. Never with advertisers.

Retention and deletion

We keep data while the connected business remains a customer, then delete or anonymize it. You may request deletion of your data at any time - see our data deletion instructions or email privacy@secondring.ca. Requests are honoured within 30 days.

Your rights

Under Canada's PIPEDA you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Text STOP to any SMS from us to opt out of messages immediately.
