Data Deletion Instructions

To request deletion of personal data held by SecondRing (including data received through Instagram or Facebook messaging):

  1. Email privacy@secondring.ca with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include the phone number, email address, or Instagram username the data relates to.
  3. We will confirm receipt within 2 business days and complete deletion within 30 days, including from backups on their normal rotation cycle.

Businesses that disconnect their Instagram account from SecondRing can additionally request deletion of all conversation history collected through that connection.
